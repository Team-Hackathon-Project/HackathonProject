Market Advisory

Self-healing scraper

Running on local rules

Add a model API key and the agent can repair selectors when a page changes, and write the advisory itself. Without one it still scans and still advises — just from the built-in rules.

How this works

  1. 1 Scan Reads the ticker, price, change and volume out of the tab you are on. If the page moved its markup, the agent rewrites the selector and carries on.
  2. 2 Read You get one call — buy, sell or hold — with a confidence reading and the reasoning behind it.
  3. 3 Decide Approve, reject or override. Every decision is recorded locally, and no order is ever placed.
