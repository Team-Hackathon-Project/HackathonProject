Why only localhost

Only localhost and 127.0.0.1 can reach the extension. That list lives in manifest.json and cannot be changed from this page — a page on any other origin is refused by the browser before the extension sees it.

Site access

Which quote pages the extension may re-read on its own.

Why background refresh needs this

Scanning a tab you are looking at needs no permission at all — that is what clicking the toolbar icon grants. Refreshing a ticker in the background is different: the page is not open, so the extension needs standing access to that one site. Nothing is granted at install, each host is asked for separately, and you can take any of them back here or from chrome://extensions.
