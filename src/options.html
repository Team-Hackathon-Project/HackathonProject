Market Advisory Engine

Settings, portfolio targets, and the self-healing selector registry.

Agent

Stored in chrome.storage.local on this machine and sent only to the provider from the extension service worker. It is never exposed to the pages you visit. Keys are kept per provider, so switching does not discard the other one. Leave it empty to run fully offline: no self-healing, and advice comes from the local rules engine.

Portfolio targets

The rules engine and the LLM both read these. Targets are what turn a quote into a signal.

Suggest targets fills the two boxes from your own data — the average of the prices you have scanned for that ticker, or your approved BUY decisions, or your average cost — with a band taken from how much those scans moved. It proposes; you still press Save. Tick automatically and every later scan of that ticker refreshes them for you.
