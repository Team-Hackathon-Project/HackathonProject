Everything you enter here is stored in chrome.storage.local on this machine. Nothing is synced, and no order is ever placed.

Getting started

Market data that
repairs itself.

Open a quote page, press one button, and get a reading you can act on — a call, the confidence behind it, and the reasoning in plain language. When the page rewrites its markup, the agent rewrites its selectors and carries on.

  • Reads the tab you are on

    No account, no data feed, no per-site integration. Whatever quote page you are looking at is the source.

  • Fixes itself when a site changes

    A broken selector is captured, sent to a model with the surrounding markup, and replaced — mid-scan, without you noticing.

  • You place every order yourself

    The extension proposes and records. It is not connected to a broker and has no way to transact.

Three moves,
in this order.

The whole product is one loop. It takes about four seconds and it ends with you.

  1. 01
    Scan

    The content script reads the ticker, price, daily change, volume and any headlines out of the live DOM, then strips the page bloat and stores structured JSON.

  2. 02
    Read

    One call — buy, sell or hold — with a confidence reading and a paragraph saying what drove it. Your own targets and cost basis are part of the input.

  3. 03
    Decide

    Approve, reject, or override with your own call and a note. Every decision is written to local storage, and nothing leaves this machine.

What happens when a site changes its markup

The lookup fails, the agent captures the parent container, asks the model for a selector that finds the live value, validates the answer against the page, and adopts it. The repair is logged, shown in the popup, and reused on every later scan of that host.

Targets are what turn a quote into a signal. Only the symbol is required — you can fill the rest in later, or let the extension suggest them once it has some history.

    Check prices in the background

    The extension re-reads each watched ticker on a timer, even with every tab closed, and notifies you when a target is crossed. Off until you ask for it.
